# For participants

You're at an event with a Rafi raffle. Here's what to expect.

## Joining

1. **Scan the QR code** on the screen, or type the short address shown under it. There's no app and no account.
2. Enter your **email** and, if you like, a **name to show on the screen**: a first name or nickname is enough.
   Leave it blank and you get a generated name like "Brave Otter", or press 🎲 to pick one. The email is only used
   to tell entries apart and to deliver prizes. It is never shown.
3. Accept the rules. Under the box, the page tells you the day your name and email are erased (a week after the
   event unless the organizer chose otherwise, at most a month; a winner's once their prize is delivered), that
   you must be 16 or older (or whatever the raffle's rules ask), and links the privacy notice, which names the
   organizer as the controller of your data.
4. Press **Join**. Your phone does a few seconds of work in the background first (a check against bots).

You'll see **You're in!** with your name on the screen and your **claim code**. If you win, you show this code to
the organizer.

## Waiting

You can close the page. Your entry is remembered on this phone for a few days, so scanning the code again or
reopening the page brings it back. If you switch browsers, entering the same email tells you your entry is safe.
It won't be moved to the new browser, so keep the first one if you can. Turn on **Keep my screen on** if you want
to watch the draw on your phone.

No smartphone? Go to the organizer: they can add you by name.

## If the organizer added you

Some organizers enter the people on their guest list or check-in list themselves, so you may be in the raffle
without scanning anything. The organizer should have told you, with links to the rules and the privacy notice.

- **What Rafi has of you:** your name, and your email if the organizer gave it. Nothing else from their list.
- **Your name is shown** on the event screen and the verification page, like everyone's, as the organizer chose:
  your full name, or your first name and initial. Two people are never shown with the same name: a namesake gets a
  number ("Anna Kovács (2)").
- **If you win**, nothing appears on a phone: watch the screen for your name, and go to the organizer, who checks
  it's you (for example, by the email you registered with). You must be in the room, as everyone must.
- **Rather not take part?** Tell the organizer (the privacy notice names whom to ask). Before the list is frozen
  for the draw, they take you off. After that, your name is hidden and your data erased when the raffle is over;
  your anonymous ID stays in the draw so it can still be verified.

## The draw

When the organizer starts the draw, registration closes and the list of entrants is published as anonymous IDs.
Then, for each prize:

- Everyone sees **"Locking in public randomness · drand round …"** and a short countdown. The result will come
  from public randomness that doesn't exist yet, so nobody, the organizer included, can know or influence it.
- The winner's name appears on the screen. On the winner's phone it's **YOU WON!** with their claim code, a chime
  and, on Android, a vibration. Everyone else sees **Not this time** and the prizes left.

**If you win, go to the organizer and show your claim code.** You must be in the room: if you're not, the prize is
drawn again and you're out. Once the organizer has checked your code, your phone says whether you take the prize
with you or the organizer sends it to you later.

Each person can win at most one prize, unless there are more prizes than people.

## Check it yourself

The participant page links to **Check the draw yourself**. The verification page re-runs every draw in your
browser from the published list and the public randomness, and shows whether everything checks out. Opened from
your phone, it also tells you that **you're in**: your entry is in the frozen list every draw was made from. Its
list of entrants lets anyone find their name. See [Verifying a draw](verifying-a-draw.md).

## Your data

Your display name appears on the screen and in the verification page's list of entrants, until your data is
erased. When you join yourself, it doesn't have to be your real name. Your email never appears. Your entry stays in the raffle until it is
finished; your name and email are erased on the day the join form told you, automatically (a winner's once their
prize is delivered), and the organizer can erase yours earlier on request once the draw is over. The **Privacy
notice** link on the page has the details.

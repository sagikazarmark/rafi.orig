# For participants

You're at an event with a Rafi raffle. Here's what to expect.

## Joining

1. **Scan the QR code** on the screen, or type the short address shown under it. There's no app and no account.
2. Enter a **name to show on the screen** (a first name or nickname is enough) and your **email**. The email is
   only used to tell entries apart and to deliver prizes. It is never shown.
3. Accept the rules and the privacy notice. The box for emails about future meetups is optional and unticked.
4. Press **Join**. Your phone does a few seconds of work in the background first (a check against bots).

You'll see **You're in!** and your **claim code**. If you win, you show this code to the organizer.

## Waiting

You can close the page. Your entry is remembered on this phone for a few days, so scanning the code again or
reopening the page brings it back. If you switch browsers, entering the same email tells you your entry is safe.
It won't be moved to the new browser, so keep the first one if you can. Turn on **Keep my screen on** if you want
to watch the draw on your phone.

No smartphone? Go to the organizer: they can add you by name.

## The draw

When the organizer starts the draw, registration closes and the list of entrants is published as anonymous IDs.
Then, for each prize:

- Everyone sees **"Locking in public randomness · drand round …"** and a short countdown. The result will come
  from public randomness that doesn't exist yet, so nobody, the organizer included, can know or influence it.
- The winner's name appears on the screen. On the winner's phone it's **YOU WON!** with their claim code, a chime
  and, on Android, a vibration. Everyone else sees **Not this time** and the prizes left.

**If you win, go to the organizer and show your claim code.** You must be in the room: if you're not, the prize is
drawn again and you're out.

Each person can win at most one prize, unless there are more prizes than people.

## Check it yourself

The participant page links to **Check the draw yourself**. The verification page re-runs every draw in your
browser from the published list and the public randomness, and shows whether everything checks out. Its published
data (`verify.json`) also lists every display name, so you can confirm you were in. See
[Verifying a draw](verifying-a-draw.md).

## Your data

Your display name may appear on the screen and on the verification page. Your email never does. The organizer
erases names and emails after the event, and you can ask them to do it sooner. The **Privacy notice** link on the
page has the details.
